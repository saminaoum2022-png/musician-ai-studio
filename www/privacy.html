Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026 · Contact: help@nabadai.com · Legal information

Who we are

NabadAi Music (“NabadAi”, “we”, “us”) is an AI music studio operated by Nabadai music For Computer Systems & Communication Equipment Software Design, a sole establishment licensed in Dubai, United Arab Emirates (trading as NabadAi). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. See also our Legal information page.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication identifiers when you sign in (for example with Google, Apple, or email/password via Supabase).
  • Profile data — username, avatar, bio, voice range label, optional voice note (“calling card”), and public profile settings you choose to share.
  • Content you create — lyrics, style prompts, uploaded photos, audio recordings (hums, vocal references, Persona voice samples), generated songs, covers, and posts you publish to Friends or your profile.
  • Usage, purchases & credits — product interactions, credit balance, redemption history, subscription or purchase status, and generation-related metadata needed to run the service.
  • Social activity — follows, likes, Echo voice moments, comments/replies, and play counts where those features are enabled.
  • Device data — app version, push-notification identifiers, coarse diagnostics, and data stored locally on your device (for example cached library and preferences).

How we use information

  • Provide and secure your account.
  • Generate, store, play back, and share music and related media you request.
  • Operate credits, support, and abuse prevention.
  • Improve reliability and fix errors.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.
  • Review public content and enforce community standards, including removing or restricting posts that violate our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

Public content & moderation

If you publish songs or posts to your public profile, information such as your username, display name, artwork, titles, captions, and audio previews may be visible to other users and appear in feeds or share links.

  • Authorized NabadAi staff and contractors may access account and content data as needed for support, billing, security, and moderation.
  • When we moderate public content (for example unpublishing a post), we may log the action, the content involved, and the team member who performed it for audit and legal compliance.
  • We may retain moderation and security logs for a limited period even after content is removed.

Third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the app. Depending on what you use, data may be processed by:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Vercel — hosting our website and API endpoints.
  • AI music providers (such as Suno) — to generate audio from your prompts and references, subject to their policies.
  • Google — if you choose “Continue with Google” for sign-in.
  • Apple — if you choose “Continue with Apple” for sign-in on iOS.
  • OneSignal — push notification delivery if you opt in (device token and account identifier; we do not send private message content in notification payloads).
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement and purchase-status management when paid plans are available.
  • Stripe — web subscription and payment processing when you purchase NabadAi Pro on nabadai.com.

These providers only receive what is needed to perform their function. We do not use an advertising or cross-app tracking SDK.

Permissions on your device

The app may ask for:

  • Microphone — record melody guides, vocal references, Echo moments, and Persona voice samples.
  • Camera & photo library — add photos to stories/moments and song artwork where you choose.
  • Notifications — send activity alerts (likes, follows, mentions, and similar) when you enable push notifications in Settings.

You can deny permissions; related features will not work until you allow them in iOS Settings.

Retention

We keep your account and content while your account is active. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we delete your authentication record and associated data in our systems, subject to short backup cycles and legal obligations.

Data stored only on your device can be removed with “Delete account” or by uninstalling the app.

Your choices & rights

  • Access and update profile information in the app.
  • Delete your account from Settings (signed-in users).
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable law provides these rights.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable law gives you that right (for example in the EU/UK).

Reporting & legal requests

To report abusive public content, copyright concerns, or privacy issues, email help@nabadai.com. We may share limited account or content information with law enforcement when required by valid legal process.

Children

NabadAi Music is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on backend systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International users

If you use the app outside your home country, your information may be processed in other countries where our providers operate.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new date at the top of this page. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

This policy is provided for transparency and App Store requirements. For legal advice specific to your situation, consult a qualified attorney. See also our Terms of Service.
