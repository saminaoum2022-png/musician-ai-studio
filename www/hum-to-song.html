Frequently asked questions

How long should my hum be?

A clear 15–30 second phrase works well. A quiet room and a steady melody help the system follow your idea.

Do I need to sing words?

No. A simple hum is enough for the melody flow; words can be added separately if you want a vocal song.

Can I upload a recording?

Yes. You can upload an existing melody guide when recording directly is not convenient.
