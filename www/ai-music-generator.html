Frequently asked questions

What is an AI music generator?

It is a creative tool that turns instructions such as lyrics, mood, style, or melody references into generated music.

Can I use my own lyrics?

Yes. You can write your own lyrics, generate a draft, edit it, and pair it with your chosen musical direction.

Can NabadAi create vocals and instrumentals?

Yes. Song creation supports vocal and instrumental directions, depending on the flow and options you choose.
