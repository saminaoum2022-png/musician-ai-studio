Help & FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help.

How do I create a song?

Tap the + Create button, then choose how you want to start — write Lyrics, Hum a melody, or use a Photo. Add a style or mood, then generate. Your finished song shows up in your Profile.

What is Persona?

Persona saves your voice so new songs can sing in your voice. Set it up once, then pick your Persona when you generate a song. You can manage it any time from Create.

How long does a song take to generate?

Usually a minute or two. You don't have to wait on the screen — you can keep browsing and we'll show the song in your Profile and Activity as soon as it's ready.

How do I publish or share a song?

Open a song from your Profile, then tap Share or Publish. Published songs can appear in Discover so other creators can hear them.

Can I remix or mashup tracks?

Yes — blend two tracks into something new, or remix to reshape a song's hook, energy, and mood. Look for the Remix and Mashup options on the player and Create screens.

What are credits?

Some actions, like generating songs, use credits. You can see your balance and plan under Settings → Credits & Plan. Promo codes can add credits too.

Who can see my profile and activity?

You're in control. Under Settings → Music presence you can turn off Show my activity or hide song titles.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. Deletion is permanent and removes your songs and profile data.

I found a bug or have a question.

We'd love to hear from you. Email help@nabadai.com for help, or support@nabadai.com to report a bug.

People you block can't message you or start a chat with you.
