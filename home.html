Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download an app?

You can try NabadAi in your browser at nabadai.com/app or install the iOS app for the best experience.

Can I use my own lyrics and voice?

Yes. Write or generate lyrics, upload a melody reference, or create a Persona from your own voice sample.

Is it free?

New accounts include free credits. Pro subscriptions add more credits and premium features.

What can I create with NabadAi?

Turn lyrics, a hum, or a photo mood into a full song — plus covers, remixes, instrumentals, and occasion templates. Explore AI music generator, hum to song, and photo to song.

Does NabadAi support Arabic music, dabke, and dialects?

Yes. Write Arabic lyrics, guide Lebanese and other dialects where supported, and shape dabke, khaleeji, tarab, and regional styles. See our Arabic AI music page.

Can I publish songs on Discover?

Yes. Keep drafts private, then publish to your profile and share on Discover when you're ready.

Are there templates for birthdays, weddings, and occasions?

Yes. In the app, pick an occasion or dedication template to get lyrics and style direction faster — useful for gifts and events.

Do I need music production experience?

No. NabadAi is built for people who start with an idea, not a DAW. You guide mood, lyrics, and style; the studio handles generation.
