Complaints

For complaints about purchases, account access, content moderation, or any service issue, email help@nabadai.com with the subject line Complaint. Include your account email, a short description, and any relevant screenshots or order details. We aim to respond within five business days.

NabadAi offers free credits to start and optional NabadAi Pro subscriptions for additional credits and features. Current pricing is shown on our homepage and in the app before you subscribe.
