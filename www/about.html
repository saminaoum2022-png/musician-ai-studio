About us

Music creation for everyone who has an idea

NabadAi is an AI music studio on web and iOS. We help you go from a hum, a lyric, or a photo mood to a finished song you can share — with real support for Arabic lyrics, dabke, Levantine folk, and regional styles.

What we believe

Start your way

Not everyone begins with a DAW or a band. Some people start with a melody in their head, a verse on their phone, or a feeling from a photo. NabadAi meets you there.

Your voice, your culture

We care about Arabic music deeply — dialects, maqam color, dabke, khaleeji, tarab, and Levantine wedding energy — not as an afterthought, but as part of the product.

Share what you make

Create privately, publish when you're ready, and connect with other musicians on Discover. Your drafts stay yours until you choose to share.

What NabadAi does

Full songs from prompts

Generate complete tracks from lyrics, style direction, hums, photo moods, covers, and remixes — then refine vocals, artwork, and metadata in one place.

Persona & voice

Save your vocal identity and reuse it across new songs so the result feels like you, not a generic demo.

Built for mobile and web

Use NabadAi in the browser or install on iPhone. Same account, same credits, same creative library when you publish.

Try the studio

Free credits to start. No credit card required for your first songs.
