Frequently asked questions

Can I edit generated lyrics?

Yes. Lyrics remain editable so you can rewrite lines, structure sections, and keep your own voice.

Does NabadAi support Arabic lyrics?

Yes. Arabic is available alongside English and other language choices, with regional music options in the creative tools.

Can I make an instrumental instead?

Yes. Choose an instrumental direction when you want music without a sung lyric.
