Support

How can we help?

For account, credits, generation, publishing, privacy, or technical questions, contact the NabadAi team.

Email help@nabadai.com

My song generation failed

Open Activity and check the generation status. Failed generations should return their credits automatically. If they do not, email support with your account email and approximate generation time.

I cannot access my account

Try the same sign-in method you used originally: Apple, Google, or email. Include the username and sign-in method in your support message, but never send your password.

How do I keep a song?

Private drafts are local to the device where you created them. Publish a finished song when you want NabadAi to keep it on your profile and make it available across devices.
